Why Choose Dezan Digitizing?

There are many embroidery digitizing services online, but not every file is prepared for real production.

At Dezan Digitizing, every design is manually digitized for its actual use — including left chest logos, hats, 3D puff, patches, and jacket backs.

We focus on clean stitching, proper stitch settings, fast turnaround, clear pricing, and support when adjustments are needed.

Manual Digitizing

Every file hand-digitized for its intended garment, zero auto-trace.

Files Stay in Your Account

Re-download previous orders whenever you need them.

Fast Turnaround

Standard orders delivered within 12–24 hours, rush ready.

Free Revisions

Complimentary adjustments until your physical stitch-out is perfect.
