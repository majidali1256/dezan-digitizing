Our Story

From a small, dedicated workshop founded over 15 years ago to becoming a global industry leader, Dezan Digitizing has been defined by one thing: uncompromising precision.

Our journey began with a passion for traditional craftsmanship and a vision to translate it into the digital era. We recognized that embroidery is not just about stitches — it's about the soul of a design.

Today, we combine decades of hands-on experience with the latest digitizing technologies to transform complex designs into flawless stitch files, ensuring every thread placement serves the art perfectly.
