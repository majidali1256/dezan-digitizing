Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Your privacy and the absolute confidentiality of your client artwork are our top priorities.

shield

Strict Artwork Confidentiality Guarantee

We never sell, distribute, display, or monetize your custom designs or logos. All uploaded artwork and stitch files remain strictly confidential and belong solely to you.

01. Overview

Dezan Digitizing ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, protect, and retain personal information and digital artwork when you interact with our website, request quotes, or purchase digitizing services.

02. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to execute your embroidery and vector orders:

Account & Contact Info

Name, company name, email address, and optional phone number used for delivery and order communications.

Project Specifications

Dimensions (width/height), placement (hat, chest, jacket back), fabric type, thread color instructions, and format requests.

Uploaded Artwork Files

Raster images (.PNG, .JPG, .JPEG), vector graphics (.AI, .EPS, .SVG, .PDF), or scan files provided for conversion.

Transaction Identifiers

Order numbers, invoice amounts, and PayPal transaction IDs. (We do NOT store credit card numbers).

03. Payment Security

All online transactions are handled through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (such as PayPal). When you make a payment, your credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials never touch our servers and are never stored in our databases.

04. Artwork Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

• Zero Resale: Your digitized files and source artwork will never be resold to other customers or apparel shops.

• Zero Unauthorized Display: We do not publish your proprietary customer logos in public galleries without your express written consent.

• Zero Third-Party Scraping: All customer file uploads are stored in encrypted cloud S3 buckets with secured access keys.

05. Cloud File Backup & Retention

As a courtesy to our embroidery clients, we retain an encrypted backup of your completed production files (.DST, .PES, .EXP, .EMB) in our cloud vaults for 5 years. If your local computer crashes, you lose your USB drive, or a customer asks for a reorder months later, you can log in to your Client Portal or email us to retrieve your files free of charge.

06. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we store about you, request corrections, or request that your files and account be permanently deleted from our database. To request data deletion, contact us directly at fdezan91@gmail.com.
