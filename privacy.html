Refund & Privacy Policy

This Refund & Privacy Policy explains the refund terms and privacy practices of Dezan Digitizing. By placing an order or using our website, you acknowledge the policies described below.

Part I

Refund Policy

01. Custom Digital Services

Dezan Digitizing provides custom digital services including embroidery digitizing and vector artwork conversion.

Because each order is created specifically according to the customer’s artwork, size, placement, and requirements, completed or started custom work is generally non-refundable.

02. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you request cancellation before work on your order has started, we may provide a full refund.

Because orders may enter production shortly after payment is received, cancellation cannot be guaranteed once work has begun.

03. Once Work Has Started

Once embroidery digitizing, vector conversion, or other custom work has begun, the order is generally non-refundable because time and labor have already been committed to creating the custom file.

This does not affect our responsibility to correct genuine problems with our work.

04. Digitizing Errors

If there is a genuine issue caused by our embroidery digitizing, we will first attempt to resolve the issue through reasonable revisions at no additional charge.

Examples may include adjustments involving:

  • Registration
  • Density
  • Pull compensation
  • Underlay
  • Stitch sequencing
  • Stitch direction
  • Trims
  • Other digitizing-related issues

Customers may be asked to provide clear photographs of the stitch-out and relevant information about the embroidery setup so that the issue can be properly evaluated.

05. Machine and Production-Related Issues

A poor embroidery result does not automatically mean that the digitized file is defective.

Embroidery results can be affected by factors outside our control, including:

  • Machine tension
  • Hooping
  • Stabilizer or backing
  • Thread type or quality
  • Needle type or condition
  • Fabric movement
  • Cap framing
  • Embroidery speed
  • Machine settings
  • Machine maintenance
  • Foam selection for 3D puff
  • Operator setup or production technique

Refunds are not normally provided for issues caused by these production factors. We will still make reasonable efforts to help identify the cause of the problem.

06. Embroidery Limitations

Embroidery has physical limitations. Extremely small text, fine lines, gradients, photographic details, complicated textures, or highly detailed artwork may need to be simplified depending on the requested embroidery size. Some details may not be possible to reproduce exactly in thread.

A refund will not normally be provided simply because all details of the original artwork cannot be retained at a physically impractical embroidery size. Increasing the embroidery size may allow additional detail to be retained.

07. Customer-Requested Changes

Free revisions are intended to correct or reasonably adjust the original digitizing work.

Additional charges may apply when a customer requests:

  • Different artwork
  • New wording
  • New design elements
  • Significant changes to the original artwork
  • A substantially different size
  • A different embroidery placement requiring re-digitizing
  • A new variation or version of the design

08. Incorrect Information Supplied by the Customer

Refunds are not normally available when the order was completed correctly based on the customer’s original instructions but the customer later discovers that incorrect information was submitted.

This may include:

  • Wrong artwork
  • Wrong size
  • Incorrect wording or spelling
  • Wrong placement
  • Incorrect file format request
  • Incomplete instructions

We will make reasonable efforts to help correct the order, but additional work may require an additional charge.

09. If We Cannot Complete Your Order

If Dezan Digitizing determines that we are unable to provide the service purchased and cannot provide an acceptable alternative, the amount paid for the affected service will be refunded.

10. Duplicate Payments

If you are accidentally charged more than once for the same order, contact us. Once verified, the duplicate payment will be refunded.

11. Bulk Production

Test Stitch Recommendation:

We strongly recommend performing a test stitch before beginning bulk embroidery production or embroidering expensive garments or hats.

Dezan Digitizing is not responsible for the cost of garments, hats, blanks, thread, materials, labor, production, or other losses resulting from bulk production performed without first testing and approving the embroidery file.

12. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will normally be returned through the original payment method whenever possible.

Payment providers such as PayPal may require additional processing time before a refund appears in the customer’s account.

13. Payment Provider Rights

This Refund Policy describes refunds provided directly by Dezan Digitizing. It does not remove any rights that may be available under applicable law or through a customer’s payment provider.

PayPal disputes, chargebacks, or other payment claims may also be subject to the payment provider’s own policies and procedures.
