Part I

Refund & Revision Policy

01. Free Unlimited Revisions

Embroidery is a precise craft influenced by fabric weight, stabilizer, and machine tension. To ensure flawless production runs, we provide 100% free unlimited revisions for up to 30 days after file delivery.

Covered under Free Revisions:

  • Density Adjustments: Calibrating density for specific fabrics (piqué polo, structured twill, flexfit caps, fleece, or canvas).
  • Pull Compensation: Eliminating gaps between satin borders and fill stitches.
  • Underlay Adjustments: Preventing stitches from sinking into lofty fabrics like jackets or towels.
  • Size Scaling: Proportional scaling up or down by up to 20% without stitch degradation.
  • Thread Trim Reduction: Optimizing paths to reduce color changes and machine run time.
  • Format Conversions: Free delivery in .DST, .PES, .EXP, .JEF, .EMB, .VP3, and any requested format.

02. Priority Revision Turnaround

All revision requests are handled via our Express Priority Queue so your commercial machines aren't kept waiting:

  • Typical Delivery: 2 to 4 hours during operational hours.
  • Guaranteed Maximum: 12 hours max.

03. 100% Money-Back Refund Guarantee

When You Are Entitled to a Full Refund:

If our digitized file fails to sew cleanly due to a digitizing error and we cannot rectify the issue after reasonable revision attempts with your sewout test photos, you are entitled to a 100% full refund.

Refunds are credited directly to your original payment method (PayPal or Credit/Debit Card) within 24 to 48 hours. No mandatory store credits.

04. Order Cancellation & Limitations

• Immediate Cancellation: You may cancel an order for an immediate 100% refund at any point before digitizing has begun.
• Artwork Swap: Submitting a completely different artwork or changing design elements after digitizing has begun requires a separate order.
• Garment Liability: Always run a test sew on scrap fabric prior to production runs. Dezan Digitizing is not liable for garment loss.
