Refund & Revision Policy

Last updated: September 2026. We stand behind every stitch. Free unlimited revisions and a complete money-back warranty if your file does not sew out cleanly.

Our Sewout Guarantee

If a digitized design has technical sewing defects (excess thread breaks, puckering, poor registration, or distorted lettering) that we cannot rectify after reasonable revision attempts, you receive a 100% full refund.

01. Free Unlimited Revisions

Embroidery is a physical craft. Factors such as garment fabric stretch, stabilizer weight, and machine tension can affect the final sewout. Therefore, we provide free unlimited revisions for up to 30 days after file delivery to make sure your embroidery looks immaculate.

What is Covered for FREE:

  • Density Calibration: Softening stitch density for thin fabrics (pique polo, tri-blend shirts) or increasing density for structured fabrics (caps, canvas, denim).
  • Pull Compensation: Adjusting push/pull distortion so circles sew perfectly round and satin borders align precisely with fill stitches.
  • Underlay Adjustments: Adding tatami or edge-walk underlay to prevent embroidery sinking into fleece, jackets, or terry cloth towels.
  • Size Scaling: Proportional resizing up or down by up to 20% without losing stitch integrity.
  • Thread Trim Reduction: Optimizing travel paths and color change sequences to minimize manual thread trimming on single-needle and multi-needle machines.
  • Format Conversion: Exporting to alternative embroidery formats (.DST, .PES, .EXP, .JEF, .EMB, .VP3, etc.) at any time.

02. Priority Revision Turnaround

We know that your embroidery machine might be scheduled or sitting on the production floor. All revision requests are placed on our Express Priority Queue:

  • Average Revision Delivery: 2 to 4 hours during regular operating hours.
  • Maximum Turnaround: 12 hours guaranteed.

03. How to Request a Revision

Requesting an edit takes less than 60 seconds:

Method 1: Client Portal (Fastest)

Log in to your Client Portal, click on the order, select "Request Revision", type your feedback, and optionally drag-and-drop a photo of your test sewout.

Method 2: Email Reply

Simply reply to your order delivery email with your Order Number and detailed feedback. Attaching a phone photo of the stitched patch helps our digitizers identify machine tension issues immediately.

04. 100% Money-Back Refund Policy

When You Are Entitled to a Full Refund:

If after 2 revision attempts our digitized file still fails to sew cleanly on your commercial embroidery machine due to a digitizing flaw, you may request a 100% full refund.

Refunds are issued directly to your original payment method (PayPal or Credit/Debit card) within 24 to 48 hours. We do not force store credit — you get real money back.

05. Exceptions & Limitations

The following scenarios are not eligible for a refund:

  • New Design / Artwork Swap: Submitting a completely different logo or drastically altered graphic after digitizing has commenced. (Changing artwork requires placing a new order).
  • Machine Hardware Faults: Physical machine malfunctions unrelated to the digitizing file (such as needle timing errors, hook damage, or incorrect tension dial calibration on the embroidery machine).
  • Client Garment Losses: As stated in industry standard guidelines, Dezan Digitizing is not liable for damaged garments or blank stock. Always test sew on scrap fabric prior to production runs.

06. Order Cancellation

You can cancel an order for an immediate full refund at any time before our digitizer starts work on the file. If work has already been completed and delivered, the order is subject to our standard Free Unlimited Revision and Quality Guarantee process.
