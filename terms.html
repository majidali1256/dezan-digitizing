05. Embroidery Limitations

Embroidery has physical limitations that differ from digital artwork. Very small text, extremely fine lines, gradients, photographic details, complex textures, closely spaced elements, or highly detailed artwork may need to be simplified to produce a practical embroidery file.

The amount of detail that can be retained depends on factors including:

  • • Final embroidery size
  • • Fabric or garment type
  • • Hat or cap construction
  • • Thread type
  • • Needle size
  • • Stitch type
  • • Design complexity

Where necessary, Dezan Digitizing may simplify extremely small or impractical details while attempting to preserve the overall appearance and recognizability of the artwork. Increasing the embroidery size may allow additional detail to be retained.

06. Embroidery Results and Production Variables

Dezan Digitizing creates files using professional embroidery digitizing practices. However, the final sewn result can also be affected by production factors outside our control.

These may include:

  • • Machine tension
  • • Thread quality or weight
  • • Needle type or condition
  • • Stabilizer or backing
  • • Hooping
  • • Fabric movement
  • • Cap framing
  • • Machine settings
  • • Machine maintenance
  • • Embroidery speed
  • • Foam selection for 3D puff
  • • Operator setup and production technique

A poor stitch-out therefore does not automatically mean that the digitized file is defective.

If you experience an embroidery problem, please provide clear photographs of the stitch-out and relevant information about the embroidery setup. We will review the issue and determine whether an adjustment to the digitized file is appropriate.

We strongly recommend performing a test stitch before:
