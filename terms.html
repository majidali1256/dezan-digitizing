You retain 100% ownership of your artwork. We provide manual digitizing and vector conversion with standard 12-24h turnaround, transparent flat-rate pricing, and free revisions to guarantee perfect sewout results.

By accessing our website (dezan-digitizing.vercel.app), submitting quote requests, uploading artwork files, or placing orders, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service and our Refund & Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please do not use our services.

02. Scope of Services

Dezan Digitizing provides custom manual embroidery digitizing (converting raster logos, graphics, and pet portraits into machine-readable stitch files such as .DST, .PES, .EXP, and .EMB) and vector art conversion (converting low-resolution images into scalable .AI, .EPS, .SVG, and .PDF formats).

All digitizing is handcrafted by experienced technicians. We do not use automated conversion algorithms that cause thread breaks or puckering.

04. Flat-Rate Pricing & Payments

  • Transparent Rates: Standard Left Chest & Hat digitizing starts at $15 flat rate. Larger designs (over 5.5 inches) start at $25. Realistic pet portraits start at $25 flat rate (up to 5.5") and $40 (large). Simple vector redraws are $15, complex vector mascot redraws are $25.
  • No Hidden Stitch-Count Fees: We charge flat rates regardless of whether your design is 8,000 stitches or 25,000 stitches.
  • Payment Processors: We accept payments securely through PayPal and major credit/debit cards. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL.

05. Turnaround Time & File Formats

Standard orders are delivered within 12 to 24 hours. Complex jacket backs and photorealistic pet portraits are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Rush orders can be completed in 4 to 8 hours upon request.

Production files are delivered in your requested machine format (.DST for Tajima/Barudan/Ricoma, .PES for Brother/Babylock, .EXP for Melco/Bernina, .EMB for Wilcom master archive, and .AI/.EPS/.SVG/.PDF for vectors). Files are also accessible in your Client Portal.

06. Test Sewout Recommendation

Always Perform a Test Sewout:

While all files are programmed by master digitizers and previewed on 3D simulators, machine tensions, needle types, bobbin threads, and backing stabilizers vary across different production machines and garments. We strongly recommend running a single test stitch-out on scrap fabric before proceeding to full production runs.

07. Revisions & Refund Policy

We offer 100% Free Unlimited Revisions for minor adjustments (density modifications, pull compensation for specific fabrics, minor size scaling up to 20%, stitch trims). For full details regarding revision turnaround and our money-back guarantee, please visit our dedicated Refund & Privacy Policy.
