Engineered for Production

The 4-Step Dezan Digitizing Standard

Bad digitizing breaks needles and puckers fabric. Our process is designed to protect your shop's run-time and maximize profitability.

01

Artwork Deconstruction

We analyze your source art for small text, gradient transitions, and color layers to establish an efficient sequencing blueprint.

02

Directional Underlay

We lay down custom edge-run, center-walk, and tatami underlays perpendicular to top stitches, locking the garment to the stabilizer.

03

Push/Pull Compensation

Fabric shrinks along stitch rows and expands across them. We calculate exact push/pull tolerances for your exact fabric choice.

04

Virtual Sewout Simulation

Every file is played through an embroidery machine simulator to verify trims, needle jumps, and zero thread-snap tension spikes.

Everything You Need to Know

Embroidery Digitizing FAQ

What file formats do I need to send you? expand_more

We accept virtually any file format: PNG, JPG, PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, or even a clear photo of an existing garment or hand drawing. We will convert your artwork into a production-ready embroidery stitch file.

How fast will I receive my finished files? expand_more

Our standard turnaround is 12 to 24 hours. If your shop is running against a strict deadline, we offer a rush service completed within 5 to 8 hours.

Do you charge extra for 3D Puff embroidery on hats? expand_more

No. Unlike many digitizing services that charge an extra $5-$10 for 3D puff, puff digitizing is included at no extra charge. Just specify that your design will be sewn over foam.

What if the sewout needs adjustments on my specific fabric? expand_more

We provide free revisions and tweaks until your machine runs it smoothly. Simply take a photo of your test sewout or describe what needs adjusting, and our digitizers will calibrate the file right away.

How small can text be in embroidery digitizing? expand_more

With standard 40-weight embroidery thread, the minimum legible letter height is approximately 4.5mm (0.18 inches). If you have smaller text, we optimize the satin column widths or recommend fine 60-weight thread settings so letters don't close up.
