Service Overview

Precision Digitizing for Structured & Unstructured Caps

Embroidery on caps is notoriously unforgiving. Unlike flat garments (t-shirts or hoodies) that stay rigid on flat frames, a cap is mounted on a curved cylindrical frame with a heavy structural seam running right down the center.

At Dezan Digitizing, every cap file is digitized from the ground up specifically for hats. We never convert a flat chest file directly onto a hat. We manually plan the stitch path from the bottom of the crown toward the top, and outward from the center seam toward the ears. This pushes excess fabric outward naturally, eliminating fabric puckering, birdnesting, and needle deflection.

Technical Specs

Cap Production Considerations

straighten

Crown Height Restrictions

Maximum recommended height is 2.25 inches (57mm) for structured mid-profile caps like Richardson 112, and 2.5 inches on high-profile trucker hats to avoid clamp collision.

sync_alt

Center-Out Sequencing

Stitching starts at the center seam and works outward to both sides simultaneously, smoothing the curved buckram and preventing needle deflection over seam ridges.

shield

Stabilizer & Tearaway

Calibrated for heavy 2.5oz to 3.0oz cap tearaway backing. Underlay stitches lock the cap buckram securely before columns are applied.

Our Advantage

Why Dezan Cap Digitizing Is Different

Most generic digitizing services use flat left-chest settings on cap orders. This results in heavy birdnesting under the needle plate, distortion at the crown peak, and crooked letters across the seam.

We calibrate every cap design specifically for curved cap hoops (Tajima, Barudan, Brother, or Melco). We embed center-to-outside sequencing, seam bridging underlay, and density relief so your embroidery machine runs fast and smooth.

Got Questions?

Cap Digitizing FAQ

Why do caps require center-out and bottom-up sequencing? expand_more

Caps are hooped on curved cylindrical frames. Standard left-to-right stitching causes the fabric to bunch at the center seam. Center-out sequencing pushes excess fabric evenly to the sides.

What is the maximum recommended height for cap embroidery? expand_more

Safe crown height is 2.25 inches (57mm) for structured hats like Richardson 112 or Yupoong 6606, and up to 2.5 inches on tall trucker hats.

Can you do 3D puff on hats for the same price? expand_more

Yes! 3D puff cap digitizing is included at our standard flat rate ($15) with zero extra fee. Just let us know you plan to sew over foam.
