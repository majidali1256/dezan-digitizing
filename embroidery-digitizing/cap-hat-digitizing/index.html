Service Overview

Why Cap Digitizing Is Different?

Caps don’t stitch like flat garments. The curved front, center seam and limited sewing area require a file that is prepared specifically for hat embroidery.

At Dezan, cap designs are planned to stitch from the bottom up and from the center outward, helping the design stay balanced as it runs across the crown. We also adjust the stitch sequence, compensation and details for the selected cap style and finished size.

A file made for left chest embroidery should not simply be reused on a hat. We prepare each cap file for the placement it will actually be embroidered on.

Got Questions?

Cap Digitizing FAQ

Why do caps require center-out and bottom-up sequencing? expand_more

Caps are hooped on curved cylindrical frames. Standard left-to-right stitching causes the fabric to bunch at the center seam. Center-out sequencing pushes excess fabric evenly to the sides.

What is the maximum recommended height for cap embroidery? expand_more

Safe crown height is 2.25 inches (57mm) for structured hats like Richardson 112 or Yupoong 6606, and up to 2.5 inches on tall trucker hats.

Can you do 3D puff on hats for the same price? expand_more

Yes! 3D puff cap digitizing is included at our standard flat rate ($15) with zero extra fee. Just let us know you plan to sew over foam.

Can I use the same embroidery file for hats and left chest? expand_more

Usually no. Hat embroidery digitizing and left chest embroidery digitizing require different stitch settings, sequencing, and push-pull construction. For the best stitch quality, a dedicated cap file is recommended.
