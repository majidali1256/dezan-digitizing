Service Overview

Cap Digitizing Made for Real Hat Embroidery

Cap embroidery is different from left-chest embroidery. Hats have a curved front, center seam, and limited stitching height, so the file needs to be planned differently.

At Dezan, every hat file is prepared for the cap style, size and placement you choose. We adjust the stitch path, spacing and compensation so your logo has a better chance of running cleanly on production.

Quality Assurance Checklist

Things We Check Before Digitizing a Hat File

straighten

Cap height limit

Most cap-front logos work best around 2.25"–2.5" tall, depending on the cap style and frame.

Center seam

If the design crosses the middle seam, the stitch path needs to be planned carefully to reduce shifting.

Small text

Tiny letters may need to be enlarged, spaced out, simplified or changed to a cleaner font.

3D Puff suitability

Puff works best with bold shapes and clean satin areas. Very thin lines and tiny details may need adjustment.

compare_arrows

Flat file vs hat file

A left-chest file should not automatically be used on a hat. Hat embroidery needs a different setup.

Got Questions?

Cap Digitizing FAQ

Why do caps require center-out and bottom-up sequencing? expand_more

Caps are hooped on curved cylindrical frames. Standard left-to-right stitching causes the fabric to bunch at the center seam. Center-out sequencing pushes excess fabric evenly to the sides.

What is the maximum recommended height for cap embroidery? expand_more

Safe crown height is 2.25 inches (57mm) for structured hats like Richardson 112 or Yupoong 6606, and up to 2.5 inches on tall trucker hats.

Can you do 3D puff on hats for the same price? expand_more

Yes! 3D puff cap digitizing is included at our standard flat rate ($15) with zero extra fee. Just let us know you plan to sew over foam.

Can I use the same embroidery file for hats and left chest? expand_more

Usually no. Hat embroidery digitizing and left chest embroidery digitizing require different stitch settings, sequencing, and push-pull construction. For the best stitch quality, a dedicated cap file is recommended.
