Common Questions

Embroidery Digitizing FAQ

What is embroidery digitizing? expand_more

Embroidery digitizing converts your artwork into a stitch file that tells an embroidery machine how to sew the design.

What embroidery file formats do you provide? expand_more

DST is our standard format. PES, EXP, JEF, VP3, CND and other formats are available on request.

Can I use the same file for a hat and left chest? expand_more

Not always. Caps often require different sequencing and compensation than flat garments, so a separate cap file may be recommended.

How long does embroidery digitizing take? expand_more

Standard turnaround is generally 12–24 hours. Complex jacket backs and detailed artwork may require additional time.

How much does embroidery digitizing cost? expand_more

Standard hat and left chest digitizing starts at $15, while larger designs over 5.5" are $25. Specialty realistic artwork has separate pricing.
