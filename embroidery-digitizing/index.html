How It Works: The 4-Step Dezan Standard

Bad digitizing breaks needles and puckers fabric. Our process guarantees high machine speeds and zero thread breaks.

01

Artwork Deconstruction

We analyze line thicknesses, color sequences, and tiny details to create a flawless stitching order.

02

Directional Underlay

Underlay stitches anchor fabric to the backing, eliminating distortion, registration gaps, and puckering.

03

Push/Pull Compensation

We balance thread tension and fabric physics so circles remain round and satin borders align perfectly.

04

Virtual Sewout Simulation

Every file runs through an embroidery simulator to verify jump counts, trim sequences, and smooth runs.

straighten Micro-Lettering Precision

We preserve small text down to 4.5mm (0.18") with standard 40wt thread, or 3.5mm with 60wt thread, preventing letter loops from closing up.

layers Fabric-Specific Calibration

Whether sewing onto 100% cotton twill, stretchy pique polos, heavy duck canvas, or fleece, we adjust density and underlays accordingly.

content_cut Smart Trim Optimization

We minimize unnecessary thread trims and jumps, saving your machine operators precious production minutes on high-volume runs.

Common Questions

Embroidery Digitizing FAQ

What file formats do I need to send you? expand_more

We accept virtually any format: JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, or clear phone photos of existing shirts and drawings. We will convert your artwork into a production-ready embroidery machine file.

How fast will I receive my completed files? expand_more

Standard turnaround is 12 to 24 hours. For rush production deadlines, we offer an expedited 5 to 8 hour rush delivery service.

Do you charge extra for 3D puff embroidery? expand_more

No. We believe in clear, flat-rate pricing. 3D puff caps and jacket files are digitized at our standard flat pricing with zero extra upcharge.

What if the sewout needs adjustments on my machine? expand_more

We provide free revisions and fine-tuning. Simply send a photo of your test sewout, and our master digitizers will calibrate the file immediately.
