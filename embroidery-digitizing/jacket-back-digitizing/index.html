Large Scale Craftsmanship

Oversized Statement Embroidery Without the Weight

A full jacket back is an imposing canvas. Spanning 10 to 14 inches and frequently containing between 60,000 and 120,000 stitches, large designs can quickly turn a comfortable jacket into an unwearable cardboard board if the digitizer simply floods the artwork with dense fills.

At Dezan Digitizing, our jacket back digitizing focuses on wearable ergonomics. We vary the stitch angles of adjacent tatami fill blocks to create light reflection and contrast without needing double stitch density. We incorporate smart jumps and color-block sorting that save 15 to 25 minutes of machine run time per jacket.

Common Questions

Jacket Back Digitizing FAQ

Do you truly charge only $25 for large jacket backs? expand_more

Yes! Any standard large jacket back over 5.5 inches is $25 flat. No hidden per-stitch fees and no surprise invoices.

What backing/stabilizer should I use for a jacket back? expand_more

For satin jackets, hoodies, or windbreakers, we recommend two layers of 2.5oz or 3.0oz cutaway backing. For heavy denim or leather vests, a single layer of heavy tearaway or specialty mesh cutaway works well.

Can you split the design if my hoop is smaller than 12 inches? expand_more

Yes. If you operate single-needle machines with maximum 8"x12" or 6"x10" hoops, we can split the file into multi-hooping sections with alignment crosshairs upon request.
