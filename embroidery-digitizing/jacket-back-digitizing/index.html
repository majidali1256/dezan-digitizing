Service Overview

Why Jacket Back Digitizing Is Different

map

Large Areas Need More Planning

A jacket back can cover a much larger area than a left chest logo. Longer stitch runs and larger filled areas need to be planned carefully so the design stays balanced during production.

tune

Density Matters More at Large Sizes

Simply enlarging a small embroidery file can create unnecessary thread buildup, stiffness or poor coverage. Large designs need to be rebuilt for the new finished size.

texture

Fabric Can Move During Long Runs

Large embroidery gives the garment more opportunity to shift while stitching. Stitch direction, sequence and compensation need to be planned with the garment and artwork in mind.

speed

Production Time Matters

Large designs usually contain more stitches and longer machine runs, so we plan color order, trims and stitch paths to keep the file practical for production.

Got Questions?

Jacket Back Digitizing FAQ

What is the standard size for a jacket back embroidery design? expand_more

Available embroidery area depends on the garment, hoop or frame, and machine setup. Most commercial jacket back designs range between 9 inches to 14 inches wide or tall to balance visibility with garment wearability.

Can you simply enlarge a left chest embroidery file to fit a jacket back? expand_more

No. Simply enlarging a small embroidery file creates excessive thread buildup, stiff bulletproof coverage, and thread breaks. Large-format designs must be reconstructed specifically for the larger finished size with calibrated stitch directions and balanced underlays.

How do you prevent a large jacket back from feeling stiff like cardboard? expand_more

We plan stitch directions and open tatami underlays with balanced density. By varying stitch angles across fill blocks, the design stays flexible and comfortable without sacrificing bold, solid color coverage.

What machine formats do you provide? expand_more

DST format is included standard. PES, EMB, EXP, JEF, VP3, and other machine formats are provided upon request at no additional charge. You also receive a complete PDF production run-sheet with color stops and stitch count.

How much does jacket back digitizing cost? expand_more

Jacket back digitizing is a transparent flat rate of $25 per design with zero hidden stitch count upcharges, even for designs exceeding 80,000 stitches.

How long does it take to digitize a jacket back? expand_more

Standard delivery is 12 to 24 hours. Rush services are also available if you have a time-sensitive production deadline.
