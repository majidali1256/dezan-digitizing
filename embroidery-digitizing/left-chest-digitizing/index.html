Got Questions?

Left Chest Digitizing FAQ

What is the standard size for a left chest embroidery logo? expand_more

Most left chest logos are commonly around 3”–4.5” depending on the artwork and garment. For circular or square logos, 2.5”–3.5” is standard, while wide rectangular logos typically measure 3.5”–4.25” wide to maintain proper proportions on polos and work shirts.

Can you digitize small lettering? expand_more

Yes. Tiny lettering may need spacing, enlargement or simplification for better embroidery results. We calibrate pull compensation, column widths, and underlays so micro text down to 4mm–5mm sews cleanly without thread bunching.

Can I use the same file for hats and left chest? expand_more

No. Hats and left chest garments have completely different physical properties. Hat embroidery requires center-out and bottom-up sequencing to handle curved buckram, while flat garments require different push-pull compensation and underlays. Using a flat file on a cap can cause center-seam puckering.

What file formats do you provide? expand_more

DST is included with every order. PES, EMB, EXP, JEF, VP3, and other machine formats are provided on request at no extra charge. You also receive a complete PDF production run-sheet with color stops and stitch count.

How much does left chest digitizing cost? expand_more

Left chest digitizing is a transparent flat rate of $15 per design. There are zero hidden fees or stitch count surcharges regardless of complexity or colors.

How long does left chest digitizing take? expand_more

Standard delivery is 12 to 24 hours. Urgent same-day rush service is also available for time-sensitive production schedules.
